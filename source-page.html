Bonus Types Explained

Here is a breakdown of every major type you will encounter at SA casinos, and what each one means for your bankroll.

Welcome / Sign-Up Bonus

The most common online casino bonus in South Africa. A welcome bonus matches a percentage of your first deposit, sometimes across multiple deposits in stages. A 100% match on R1,000 gives you R1,000 in bonus money to play with. These bonuses almost always carry wagering requirements, so the bonus amount is not immediately withdrawable. They suit players making their first deposit at a new casino and wanting extra funds to explore the platform. If you are comparing welcome offers, an online casino bonus in South Africa is almost always this type.

No Deposit Bonus

A no-deposit bonus in South Africa used to be among the rarer offers you will encounter, but that’s changed and they are now a standard feature at most casinos. It allows you to play without making a deposit first, which sounds ideal, but wagering requirements still apply and are often higher than those attached to standard welcome bonuses. A no deposit bonus in South Africa will also typically come with a lower maximum withdrawal limit than a standard welcome offer. They suit players who want to try a casino with zero financial commitment, though cashing out from them is less straightforward than it appears.

Free Spins

Free spins are always tied to specific slot titles and are not freely usable across all games on the platform. You can’t use free spins on table games or live casino titles because they're single-play rounds exclusively for slot machines. Winnings generated from free spins are typically credited as bonus money you must wager a set number of times before you can withdraw it, not as cash you can take out straight away. They suit slots for players who want extra rounds on a particular game without spending their own balance.

Reload Bonus

A reload bonus works like a welcome bonus but applies to repeat deposits after your first. If you only deposit occasionally, reload bonuses will rarely apply to you. They are designed for regular players who deposit consistently. Match percentages are typically lower than welcome offers, with 50% being common, but the value adds up over time if you play frequently.

Cashback

Cashback paid as real cash with no wagering requirements is the most player-friendly version of this bonus and the one worth seeking out. It is less common than cashback credited as bonus money you must wager before withdrawing, but significantly more valuable. Both refund a percentage of what you lost overall during a set period, typically weekly. If you have losing weeks regularly, cashback is one of the few bonus types that works in your favour. You do not need to win anything for it to pay out. Always check which type applies before factoring cashback into your decision.

VIP / Loyalty Bonus

VIP bonuses are invite-only and not available to all players. They typically include a personal account manager, higher deposit and withdrawal limits, faster processing times, and exclusive promotions not listed publicly. If you are invited to join a VIP programme, the first things to check are withdrawal speed and the wagering requirements on exclusive offers. These vary significantly between operators and are usually negotiable at this level.

Golden Chips

Golden chips are table game chips issued as a bonus. Golden chips are granted in fixed promotional allocations or sets (e.g., 5 chips worth R10 each) to use on qualifying table games. Winnings may be paid as cash or as bonus money depending on the casino terms, and that difference is worth checking before you use them. They suit table game players specifically and are less common than free spins or match bonuses. 

Casino Bonus Codes

Some casinos use bonus codes to unlock one-off offers that are not automatically applied at sign-up. These can apply with or without a deposit depending on the promotion. If a bonus code is required, enter it during registration or at the cashier before depositing, as entering it afterwards usually means missing the offer entirely.

How to Evaluate a Bonus

Five factors separate a bonus worth claiming from one that looks good on paper but is difficult to clear in practice. Understanding wagering requirements in South Africa is where most players go wrong, so start there.

Wagering Requirement

The wagering requirement tells you how many times you must bet the bonus amount before withdrawing. The SA market average sits between 15x and 30x in South Africa, and anything below 20x is strong. A R1,000 bonus at 30x means placing R30,000 in total wagers before a single rand of bonus winnings becomes withdrawable. This is the single most important figure to check when comparing casino bonuses South Africa players are considering. Wagering requirements in South Africa tend to run higher than in other markets, so always read this figure before anything else.

Bonus Percentage and Cap

A 200% bonus sounds better than 100%, but these are still subject to the limitations of caps. A bonus capped at R500 means the maximum bonus amount you can receive is R500, regardless of the match percentage (a 200% match on a R250 deposit reaches the R500 cap). Always read both the percentage and the maximum amount together before making a decision.

Expiry Period

Bonuses expire if you do not meet the wagering requirements within a set timeframe, with 14 to 30 days being typical. If you cannot realistically play enough within that window, a smaller bonus with a longer expiry will serve you better than a larger one with a tight deadline. When two offers look similar, the expiry period is often the tiebreaker worth checking.

Redeemable vs Non-Redeemable

A redeemable bonus means both the bonus money and any winnings become withdrawable once requirements are met. A non-redeemable bonus means only the winnings can be withdrawn and the bonus itself stays with the casino. Non-redeemable bonuses are more common than most players expect, particularly on no-deposit offers. Always check which type you are being offered before claiming. If a bonus turns out to be non-redeemable, shift your focus to maximising your winnings rather than chasing the bonus amount itself. The bonus money was never going to be yours to keep anyway.

Highlight: When comparing any welcome bonus casino in South Africa, always check redeemability first. A non-redeemable bonus at 20x wagering can be worth less than a redeemable bonus at 30x, depending on the cap.

How Much Your Bets Count Toward Clearing the Bonus

Slots typically contribute 100% toward clearing wagering requirements in South Africa. Blackjack and roulette commonly contribute only 5% to 10%, which means your bets count for a fraction of their face value when clearing a bonus. If you prefer table games, this is the most important factor on the list. A generous online casino bonus in South Africa with low table game contribution can take ten times longer to clear than a smaller bonus with full contribution.

Pre-Wager vs After-Wager Bonuses

The timing of when a bonus is credited changes how you should approach your session from the first spin.

A pre-wager bonus is credited immediately and playable upfront (with wagering requirements attached before withdrawal). An after-wager bonus requires you to complete the wagering requirements using your real money balance before the bonus funds are released to your account.

Most SA casinos allow only one active bonus at a time. If you claim a second offer before completing the first, it will either queue behind the current one or require you to forfeit the existing bonus before the new one activates. Always check this before opting into a second promotion.

Payment Methods and Bonuses

Your choice of payment method can determine whether you receive a bonus at all, so this is worth confirming before you deposit.

Not every payment method triggers a welcome bonus. Some are explicitly excluded from bonus eligibility in the terms, and using one of these means the bonus is simply not applied with no way to retroactively claim it. 1Voucher is widely accepted at SA casinos and is generally eligible for bonus play, making it a practical option for players who prefer not to use bank cards online. Real-time, digital bank payment systems such as Capitec Pay and PayShap are often included for bonus eligibility at certain operators, so check the terms to see if they are before you use them.

Also confirm that your chosen deposit method supports withdrawals. Some methods are deposit-only, which means you will need a separate method to cash out and this can delay your withdrawal by several days. See the full payment methods guide for a breakdown by the operator. 

Getting Started

Follow these steps to make sure the bonus applies correctly, and no offer is missed.

  1. Choose a casino from the comparison table above. 
  2. Check the bonus terms before registering; like the wagering requirement, how much your bets count toward clearing it, expiry period, and maximum bet per round while a bonus is active. 
  3. Register and enter any required bonus code at sign-up, not afterwards. 
  4. Deposit using a bonus-eligible payment method and confirm your chosen option is not excluded before you proceed. 
  5. Claim your bonus. Some casinos apply it automatically while others require you to activate it yourself through the cashier or promotions page. 
  6. Play through the wagering requirement on eligible games, keeping in mind how much each game type counts toward clearing it. 
  7. Withdraw once requirements are fully met.

FAQ

The questions below cover the details most players miss before claiming a bonus. If you are comparing offers for the first time, the wagering and redeemability answers are the most important ones to read. 

  • Do I need to deposit to claim a casino bonus?

    Not always. No-deposit bonuses let you play without putting money in first, and these are common at most SA casinos, Wagering requirements still apply. Just remember that most bonuses, including welcome offers, require a deposit to trigger them.

  • How do I know if a bonus is worth claiming?

    Check the wagering requirement first. If it is above 40x, the bonus is hard to clear. Below 20x is strong. Also check whether the bonus is redeemable. If it is not, only your winnings can be withdrawn, not the bonus itself. 

  • Can I withdraw my bonus straight away?

    No. Every bonus comes with wagering requirements you must meet before withdrawing. The only exception is cashback paid as real cash, which some casinos offer with no conditions attached.

  • Can I use a casino bonus on blackjack or roulette?

    You can play, but table games typically count only 5–10% toward clearing the bonus. That means it takes far longer to meet the requirements than if you played slots. Some casinos exclude table games from bonus play entirely, so always check before you start.

  • Which payment method should I use to claim a bonus?

    Bank cards and 1Voucher are the most reliably eligible options at SA casinos. EFT, Ozow, PaySlik, voucher systems (1Voucher, OTT, Blu Voucher), and Capitec Pay are the most commonly accepted payment methods. Check the bonus terms before depositing to make sure your chosen method qualifies. 

  • What happens if I run out of time to meet the wagering requirements?

    The bonus and any winnings attached to it are forfeited. Your own deposited funds are not affected. Some casinos will extend the deadline if you contact support before it expires, though this is not guaranteed.
